The Bundle Tray

    Color balanceEmpty tray

    The tray takes five items from anywhere on this page — the seasonal desk, the shelves, even a whole ready-made set. It keeps a running total, applies 10% at three pieces and 15% at five, and watches your color balance so your set doesn't end up entirely one ink.

    1. 01Tap Add on anything in the catalogue. Tapes count as one, fountain pens count as one, your second-guessing counts for two.
    2. 02Watch the tray: five slots, a color-balance bar, and a total that updates as you go. Swap freely until it feels right.
    3. 03Three pieces save 10% · five pieces save 15% · and anything over $45 ships free, wrapped in kraft.
