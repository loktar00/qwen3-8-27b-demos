faq
Can I switch plans or billing cycles later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately with prorated billing; downgrades take effect at the next cycle. Switching from monthly to annual also prorate-credits the current month.

What happens if I exceed my API call limit?

Nothing breaks. We meter the overflow at a published per-1k rate and flag it in your dashboard. Two warnings, then a prompt to upgrade — we never hard-silence traffic without notice.

Do you offer a free tier or trial?

Every paid plan starts with a 14-day trial, no card required. After that the plan either activates or the workspace moves to read-only until you choose a tier.
